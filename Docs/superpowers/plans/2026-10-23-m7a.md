authored takeoff points or target-platform action labels drive training.

   preparation and landing gates. Training-only resets; never modify game scenes.

   time, jump, collision and reversal costs. Record episode outcomes and seeds.
5. Build training executable and perform actual PPO training. Compare untrained
   and trained held-out success, collisions and unnecessary jumps. Export ONNX.
6. Verify local inference in Unity Play Mode, preserve Legacy baseline, report
   failures and incomplete curriculum honestly. Commit only task-owned files.
